# Adoption and External Use

The U.S. Small Business Capital Access Atlas welcomes genuine use in research, teaching,
economic-development analysis, data engineering, and reproducibility work.

## Ways to use the Atlas

You can:

- use the public Streamlit application;
- clone or fork the repository;
- run the Jupyter examples;
- reuse the Python research utilities under the MIT License;
- reproduce state maps and data-quality diagnostics;
- extend the public-data connectors; and
- cite the software in research or teaching.

## Report verified use

If you use the project, open a **Research / Adoption Report** issue and describe:

- your research, course, organization, or project context;
- which component you used;
- what worked;
- limitations you encountered; and
- a public paper, repository, report, syllabus, or demonstration link when available.

Do not post confidential business records, applicant information, proprietary data, or
personal data.

## What counts as meaningful outside use

Examples include a derivative repository, citation, pull request, reproducible issue,
public class project, independent replication, or documented organizational test.

Stars and unsupported testimonials are not treated as evidence of impact.
