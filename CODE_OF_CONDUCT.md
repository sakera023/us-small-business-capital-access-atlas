# Community Conduct

This repository welcomes good-faith research and technical participation.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss technical and methodological disagreements respectfully;
- critique code, evidence, assumptions, and claims rather than people;
- protect confidential and personally identifiable information;
- disclose important methodological limitations;
- avoid fabricated data, citations, adoption claims, or results;
- respect software licenses and public-data terms; and
- use the security policy for sensitive vulnerability reports.

Maintainers may close or remove contributions that expose sensitive information, misstate
data provenance, or violate these standards.
