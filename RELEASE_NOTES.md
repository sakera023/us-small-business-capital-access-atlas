# U.S. Small Business Capital Access Atlas v0.2.0

Version 0.2.0 is the first full professional research-software release of the Atlas.

## Highlights

### Two authoritative U.S. public-data layers

- U.S. Small Business Administration State Small Business Statistics 2025
- U.S. Census Bureau County Business Patterns 2023 state file

The Atlas keeps source provenance, reference period, geographic meaning, and
interpretation boundaries visible.

### Expanded public dashboard

The live Streamlit Atlas now includes:

- SBA national maps;
- Census CBP establishments, employment, and payroll maps;
- state comparisons;
- downloadable clean state-level tables;
- Capital Access Opportunity Index Lab;
- data-quality diagnostics; and
- leave-one-metric-out sensitivity analysis.

### Reproducible research workflow

This release adds:

- four Jupyter notebooks;
- three documented geographic case studies;
- automated public-data validation;
- machine-readable validation artifacts;
- tested data-quality utilities;
- index robustness diagnostics; and
- Python 3.11/3.12 CI.

### Research integrity and citation

The repository includes:

- CITATION.cff;
- CITATION.md with BibTeX;
- CodeMeta metadata;
- data-governance documentation;
- validation methodology;
- research impact/adoption framework;
- community conduct standards; and
- professional issue and pull-request templates.

## Responsible-use boundary

The Atlas analyzes aggregate geographic public data. It is not an applicant-level
underwriting system and should not be used to make individual lending, investment, credit,
or eligibility decisions.

Geographic associations and composite rankings do not by themselves establish causation,
discrimination, or policy effectiveness.
