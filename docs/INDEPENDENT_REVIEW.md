# Independent Methodological Review Package

## Purpose

This package is designed for an **independent external reviewer** who did not author the
Atlas methodology.

The maintainer must not self-complete this review or pre-fill favorable conclusions.

## Materials to review

1. [Index Methodology](INDEX_METHODOLOGY.md)
2. [Validation and Robustness](VALIDATION.md)
3. [Validation Report](VALIDATION_REPORT.md)
4. [Data Sources](DATA_SOURCES.md)
5. [Data Governance](DATA_GOVERNANCE.md)
6. `src/capital_access_atlas/indicators.py`
7. `src/capital_access_atlas/analysis.py`

## Review questions

Please evaluate:

- construct validity of the proposed dimensions;
- whether indicators are conceptually distinct;
- appropriateness of percentile/z-score/robust transformations;
- weighting assumptions;
- inverse-direction handling;
- missing-data treatment;
- coverage thresholds;
- risk of double counting correlated indicators;
- geographic scale and ecological-fallacy risks;
- temporal/source-vintage alignment;
- interpretation and responsible-use language; and
- what evidence would be required before publishing a formal index.

## Recommended reviewer output

A reviewer may submit:

- a signed or attributable review memorandum;
- a public GitHub issue comment;
- a pull request with methodological corrections; or
- a public report/repository that independently evaluates the method.

The review should identify the reviewer's relevant expertise and any conflicts of interest.

## Independence standard

The Atlas will only describe this milestone as **independent review completed** when the
feedback comes from a genuine third party and is retained as verifiable evidence.

A favorable conclusion is not required. Critical feedback is valuable research evidence
when it is specific and methodologically grounded.
