# Research Outreach Kit

## Purpose

These templates are intended to generate **genuine independent use and review**, not
endorsement on request.

## 1. Independent replication invitation

**Subject:** Invitation to independently reproduce an open U.S. small-business capital-access Atlas

Hello,

I maintain an open-source research project, the U.S. Small Business Capital Access Atlas,
which integrates SBA, U.S. Census County Business Patterns, Census TIGERweb, and CDFI Fund
public data.

I am inviting independent researchers and data practitioners to reproduce the public-data
validation workflow and report any discrepancies or methodological concerns. The
repository includes a short replication protocol, tests, data-provenance documentation,
and machine-readable validation outputs.

Repository:
https://github.com/sakera023/us-small-business-capital-access-atlas

Public application:
https://capital-access-atlas-sakera.streamlit.app/

Critical feedback is welcome; no favorable conclusion is expected.

Best,
Sakera Begum

## 2. Teaching / classroom use invitation

**Subject:** Open-source U.S. small-business public-data project for classroom use

Hello,

I am sharing an open-source geospatial research project that may be useful for courses in
data science, business analytics, economic development, or responsible analytics.

Students can reproduce state/county public-data workflows, inspect source provenance,
build maps, and test how composite rankings change under alternative normalization,
weighting, and missing-data assumptions.

If you use or evaluate it in a course, I would appreciate a brief public or attributable
use report describing what was useful and what should be improved.

Repository:
https://github.com/sakera023/us-small-business-capital-access-atlas

Best,
Sakera Begum

## 3. Economic-development practitioner invitation

**Subject:** Request for practitioner feedback on an open capital-access geography Atlas

Hello,

I am seeking practitioner feedback on an open-source U.S. Small Business Capital Access
Atlas that combines SBA, Census CBP, county boundary, and Certified CDFI public data.

The current project is descriptive and does not make applicant-level lending decisions. I
would value feedback on which geographic indicators are useful, which are misleading, and
what additional public data would make the tool more relevant to real economic-development
analysis.

Repository:
https://github.com/sakera023/us-small-business-capital-access-atlas

Public application:
https://capital-access-atlas-sakera.streamlit.app/

Best,
Sakera Begum

## Evidence rule

An outreach message is **not** evidence of impact by itself. Evidence begins when an
independent person or organization actually uses, reviews, reproduces, cites, teaches
with, or publicly extends the Atlas.
