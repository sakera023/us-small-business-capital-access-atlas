# Research Questions

The Atlas is designed to support transparent, reproducible investigation of questions such
as:

1. Which states have the highest and lowest concentrations of small-business activity?
2. How do small-business employment and job-creation indicators vary geographically?
3. Where does business activity appear strong relative to community-development finance
   infrastructure?
4. How do rural and metropolitan capital-support environments differ?
5. How sensitive are place-based rankings to indicator choice and weighting?
6. Which state-level patterns remain stable when the analysis moves to county-level data?
7. How do business structure, labor-market conditions, and regional income relate to
   capital-access opportunity?
8. Where are data gaps large enough that a composite score should not be reported?

These questions are exploratory. Geographic associations should not be interpreted as
causal findings without an appropriate research design.
